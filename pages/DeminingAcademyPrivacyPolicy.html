Privacy Policy for Demining Academy VR

Effective Date: August 2026

Last Updated: August 2026

TheDeiw's Games ("we", "us", or "our") operates and offers the Demining Academy VR application (the "App") for virtual reality headsets. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding any data processed through our App.

1. Information Collection and Processing

Demining Academy VR does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII) to external servers operated by TheDeiw's Games.

All gameplay data (such as user settings, simulation progress, and local achievements) is processed and stored locally on your VR headset device.

2. Third-Party Services and Platform Telemetry

While we do not collect personal data directly, the App relies on third-party frameworks and platforms to ensure correct operation, performance optimization, and platform distribution:

  • Meta / Horizon Platform: Distributed via Meta Quest Store. Meta may collect platform telemetry, hardware specifications, crash logs, and operating metrics according to Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Unity Engine: Developed using Unity. Unity may collect basic diagnostic metrics (e.g., device model, operating system version, system language) for stability and runtime diagnostics. Please review Unity's Privacy Policy for more details.

3. Data Deletion Rights and Instructions

Because we do not store any user data on our own external servers, we do not maintain online user accounts or remote database records to delete.

How to delete your data:

  • Local Save Data: You can delete all locally saved game progress, settings, and local data at any time by uninstalling the App from your Meta Quest headset or clearing the app's local data via the Meta Quest device settings.
  • Platform Data Requests: If you wish to delete data collected by Meta or Unity, please submit your request directly to the respective third-party service providers.
  • Contact Request: If you have any questions or wish to confirm that no personal data is stored by us, you can submit a data deletion inquiry by emailing us at Chehovskyi150@gmail.com.

4. Children’s Privacy

Demining Academy VR is intended for users who comply with Meta Quest platform age requirements. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has inadvertently provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately, and we will take steps to ensure such information is promptly removed.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
