Privacy Policy for Turret Rush

Effective Date: November 2026

Last Updated: November 2026

TheDeiw's Games ("we", "us", or "our") offers the Turret Rush mobile game (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding any data processed through our App.

1. Information Collection and Processing

Turret Rush does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII). The App has no user accounts, no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no analytics or tracking services of our own.

Any gameplay data (such as settings or progress) stays on your device.

2. Device Permissions

The App does not request access to any features or data of your device. In particular, it does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, files, or phone and messaging functions. The only input it needs is your touch on the screen to aim the turret.

3. Third-Party Services and Platform Telemetry

While we do not collect personal data directly, the App relies on third-party software and platforms:

  • Unity Engine: Developed using Unity. Unity may collect basic diagnostic metrics (e.g., device model, operating system version, system language) for stability and runtime diagnostics. Please review Unity's Privacy Policy for more details.
  • Distribution platforms: If you download the App from a third-party store or platform (such as itch.io), that platform may collect data according to its own policy, for example itch.io's Privacy Policy. We do not receive this data.

4. Data Deletion

Because we do not store any user data on our own servers, there is no online account or remote record to delete.

  • Local data: You can delete everything the App stores on your device at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its data in your device settings.
  • Platform data: If you wish to delete data collected by Unity or a distribution platform, please submit your request directly to that provider.
  • Contact request: If you have any questions or wish to confirm that no personal data is stored by us, you can email us at Chehovskyi150@gmail.com.

5. Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has inadvertently provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately, and we will take steps to ensure such information is promptly removed.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
